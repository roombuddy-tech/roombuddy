How it works

Book a room in 3 steps

No brokerage. No long-term lock-in. No hidden charges. Just a place to stay.

1

Search

Enter your city and dates. Filter by price, food, AC, bathroom type and gender preference. See the actual room photos and flatmate profiles before you do anything.

2

Book & pay

Pick the room you like. Pay securely via UPI, card or netbanking — no deposit wire transfers, no cash. Your booking is confirmed instantly or within hours.

Check in & stay

Turn up, meet your flatmates, cook a meal. Everything is set up — bed, WiFi, kitchen. Feel right at home from the moment you walk in.
