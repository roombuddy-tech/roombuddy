How it
works

i.

Search & message

Filter by area, budget, dates, food, AC and bathroom. Real photos and real flatmate profiles. Message the host for free.

ii.

Request to book: ₹49

Pay the flat fee by UPI or card. That's all we charge. Rent goes straight to your host.

iii.

Host confirms, you move in

Hosts usually reply within 24 hours. If they decline or don't respond, your ₹49 comes back automatically.

i.

Find the flat, and the people

Browse long-term rooms and see who already lives there: their profiles, hometowns and how the household runs.

ii.

Talk it through

Message free in the app, or unlock the number for ₹29. Visit, ask questions, and make sure it feels right.

iii.

Agree and move in

Settle rent and deposit directly with the flat. No brokerage, ever, and no middleman holding your money.

i.

List the room, free

Add photos, the rent, who lives there, and whether it's a short stay, a permanent spot, or both.

ii.

Get verified

A quick Aadhaar check, and your listing goes live. It stays live until you pause it, so there's no reposting every day.

iii.

Choose who moves in

Chat with verified guests, see their profiles, and accept the right person, whether they're a guest for the month or a flatmate for years.
