The problem

Finding a room in a shared
home is still guesswork.

Thousands of rooms get sublet across India every month. The demand has always been there. A place built for it hasn’t.

01

Nowhere to actually search

Scattered across group chats and classifieds. No filters, no dates. You scroll and hope.

02

Listings vanish in hours

Post today, buried by tomorrow. So you post again. And again.

03

Conversations get lost

Twelve people, three apps. By Thursday you’ve lost the one who was actually right.

04

You can't tell who's real

A stranger is moving into your home. You have a photo and a first name.

05

Nothing is comparable

Rent, deposit, what’s included — buried in replies, if mentioned at all.

06

So both sides give up

Hosts stop posting. Guests stop looking. The room sits empty anyway.

Nobody built for this, because there's no brokerage in it.

Portals chase whole flats and the commission that comes with them. Short-stay sites chase tourists. One spare room in a real home was never worth anyone’s time.
