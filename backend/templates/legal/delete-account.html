Delete your RoomBuddy account

We're sorry to see you go. You can delete your account in two ways:

Option 1 — From the app

  1. Open the RoomBuddy app
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Tap "Delete my account"
  4. Confirm the deletion

Option 2 — Via email

Send an email to support@roombuddy.co.in with the subject "Delete my account" and include your registered phone number. We'll process your request within 48 hours.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • Your profile information is permanently removed
  • Any pending bookings are cancelled
  • Your listings (if host) are deactivated
  • Your reviews remain but are anonymized
  • This action cannot be undone
