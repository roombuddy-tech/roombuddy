1. Introduction

RoomBuddy ("RoomBuddy," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use the RoomBuddy mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Platform, including Hosts (who list rooms), Guests (who book rooms), and visitors. By accessing or using the Platform, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We process personal information in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), as applicable.

2. Who Controls Your Data

RoomBuddy, a company registered in India, is the data fiduciary (controller) responsible for your personal information. This means we determine the purposes and means of processing your personal data collected through the Platform.

For payment-related data, our payment partner Razorpay acts as an independent controller for information processed through their payment infrastructure, subject to their own privacy policy.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you provide directly when using the Platform:

Category Examples
Account information Phone number, first and last name, email address, city, gender, profile photo
Identity verification Aadhaar number (for KYC verification), email verification data
Listing information Property details, room descriptions, photos, pricing, house rules, amenities, location
Booking information Check-in/check-out dates, number of guests, meal preferences, special requests

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Platform, we automatically collect certain information:

  • Device information — device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, app version, and crash reports.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, searches performed, listings viewed, booking history, and interaction patterns.
  • Location information — approximate location based on your IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location without your explicit consent.
  • Log data — IP address, access times, referring URLs, and browser or app type.

3.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from third-party sources, including:

  • Payment partner (Razorpay) — transaction confirmation, payment status, and payment method details (we do not receive or store your full card number).
  • Identity verification providers — Aadhaar verification status and results from our KYC partner.
  • OTP and SMS providers (MSG91) — delivery status of OTPs and transactional messages.

4. How We Use Your Information

4.1 Providing the Platform

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Verify your identity through phone OTP, email, and Aadhaar KYC.
  • Enable you to create listings, search for rooms, and make bookings.
  • Facilitate communication between Hosts and Guests.
  • Process payments and payouts through Razorpay.
  • Send transactional notifications (booking confirmations, payment receipts, reminders).
  • Provide customer support.

4.2 Safety, Security, and Trust

We use your information to:

  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Verify the identity and trustworthiness of Members.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and community policies.
  • Investigate and respond to reports of misconduct or safety concerns.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Platform.

4.3 Improving the Platform

We use your information to:

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features and user experience.
  • Conduct research and development on new features.
  • Fix bugs and resolve technical issues.
  • Measure the effectiveness of our services.

4.4 Communications

We use your information to:

  • Send service-related notices that are necessary for Platform operation (non-marketing).
  • Send promotional communications about new features, offers, or updates, where you have opted in or where permitted by applicable law. You can opt out at any time through notification preferences in the app.

4.5 Legal Obligations

We use your information to comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or governmental requests, including tax reporting obligations and responses to law enforcement.

5. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

5.1 Between Members

When a booking is confirmed, we share limited information between the Host and Guest to facilitate the stay. This includes:

  • Shared with the Host: Guest's first name, profile photo, and booking details (dates, number of guests).
  • Shared with the Guest: Host's first name, profile photo, property address (after booking), and contact information for check-in coordination.

5.2 Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:

  • Razorpay — payment processing and payout services.
  • MSG91 — OTP delivery and transactional SMS.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — cloud infrastructure, storage, and hosting.
  • Email service provider (Amazon SES) — transactional and verification emails.
  • Sentry — error tracking and monitoring (anonymized data only).

These service providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and may only use it to perform the services we have engaged them to provide.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, or in response to valid requests by public authorities (such as a court order, tax authority, or law enforcement), including to meet national security or law enforcement requirements.

5.4 Safety and Protection

We may disclose your information where we believe it is necessary to investigate, prevent, or take action regarding illegal activities, suspected fraud, situations involving potential threats to the safety of any person, or violations of our Terms of Service.

5.5 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, your personal information may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you of any such change and the choices you may have regarding your information.

6. Payment Information

All payments on the Platform are processed by our payment partner, Razorpay. When you make a payment:

  • Your payment card details are collected and processed directly by Razorpay. RoomBuddy does not receive or store your full card number, CVV, or other sensitive payment credentials.
  • We receive only transaction confirmation data (amount, status, payment ID, and last four digits of the card, if applicable) necessary to maintain booking records.
  • Razorpay's handling of your payment information is subject to their own Privacy Policy.

Razorpay is PCI-DSS compliant, ensuring your payment data is handled with the highest security standards.

7. Cookies & Tracking

The RoomBuddy mobile app does not use browser cookies. However, we may use the following technologies:

  • Local storage — we store authentication tokens and user preferences securely on your device using encrypted local storage (AsyncStorage with secure key management).
  • Analytics — we may collect anonymous, aggregated usage data to understand how the Platform is used and to improve our services. This data cannot be used to identify individual users.
  • Push notifications — with your permission, we send push notifications for booking updates, messages, and promotional content. You can manage notification preferences in the app settings or your device settings.

If we launch a web-based platform in the future, we will update this section with details about cookie usage and provide appropriate consent mechanisms.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. Specific retention periods are as follows:

  • Account data — retained for the lifetime of your account plus 180 days after deletion to allow for account recovery and to resolve pending disputes.
  • Booking records — retained for 7 years to comply with Indian tax and accounting regulations.
  • Payment transaction records — retained for 7 years as required under the Income Tax Act, 1961 and GST regulations.
  • Identity verification data (Aadhaar) — we store only the verification status (verified/not verified), not the Aadhaar number itself. Verification is performed through our KYC partner and the raw data is not retained by RoomBuddy.
  • OTP records — automatically deleted within 24 hours of generation.
  • Communication logs — retained for 1 year after the relevant booking is completed.
  • Usage and analytics data — retained in anonymized/aggregated form indefinitely for analytical purposes.

When your personal information is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize it so that it can no longer be associated with you.

9. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption in transit — all data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS).
  • Encryption at rest — sensitive data stored on our servers is encrypted using AES-256 encryption.
  • Authentication security — passwords are never stored in plain text. JWT tokens are used for session management with short-lived access tokens and secure refresh token rotation.
  • Infrastructure security — our servers are hosted on AWS (ap-south-1, Mumbai region) with VPC isolation, security groups, and regular security patches.
  • Access controls — access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.
  • OTP security — OTPs are hashed before storage and are valid for a limited time with attempt limits to prevent brute-force attacks.
Note: While we take all reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. If you become aware of any security breach, please contact us immediately.

10. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection laws, including the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to access — you may request a summary of the personal information we hold about you and the processing activities carried out with it.
  • Right to correction — you may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information. You can update most information directly through the app's Edit Profile section.
  • Right to erasure — you may request deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal retention obligations. You can initiate account deletion through the app or by contacting support.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.
  • Right to grievance redressal — you may raise concerns with our Grievance Officer (see section 14 below) or file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Right to nominate — you may nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity, as provided under the DPDP Act.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@roombuddy.co.in or through the in-app support feature. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

11. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at privacy@roombuddy.co.in, and we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

If processing personal data of children becomes necessary in the future, we will obtain verifiable parental consent as required under the DPDP Act, 2023.

12. International Data Transfers

Your personal information is primarily stored and processed in India on servers hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the Mumbai (ap-south-1) region.

In limited circumstances, your data may be accessed by service providers located outside India (for example, for global services like error monitoring or email delivery). In such cases:

  • We ensure that the recipient provides a level of data protection comparable to Indian data protection standards.
  • We use contractual safeguards (such as data processing agreements) to protect your information.
  • We comply with any conditions or restrictions imposed by the Central Government of India regarding cross-border data transfers under the DPDP Act.
